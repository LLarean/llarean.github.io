**Heroes and Dice** is a small mobile dice battler made with Unity: roll five dice, keep what helps, reroll the rest, and beat ten enemies on the way to the dragon. Portrait, one hand, short sessions. Work in progress — playable from start to finish, not released yet.

Every die has four faces: attack, defense, heal and empty. Roll, hold what you need (up to three tries per turn), then the hero heals, gains armor and strikes; the enemy strikes back. Five or more attack faces in one roll is a critical hit.

- Campaign: ten enemies from the Small Slime to the Dragon, reward dice after each victory, three heroes (knight, archer, mage), New Game+ after the dragon
- Special dice: Sharp, Reliable, Thorny, Vampiric, Joker, Golden, Last Chance, Try Die, Extra Die — each changes what its own roll is worth
- Tournament: a bracket against a bot that plays by the same rules
- Eight languages: English, German, Russian, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese
- Code under MIT; CI builds Android, WebGL and Windows
