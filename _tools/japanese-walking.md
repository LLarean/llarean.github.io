**Japanese Walking** is a free, open-source interval walking timer built with Flutter: 3 minutes fast, 3 minutes slow, 5 times — a 30-minute session. It runs in the browser and on Android, speaks English and Russian, and plays a sound when the pace changes.

- On Android, plays your own music for each pace, with cross-fades between them
- Keeps going with the screen off via a media session
- Sound and vibration cues on phase change (vibration can be turned down or off)
- Light and dark theme that follows the phone until you pick one
- Part of the [interval-apps](https://github.com/manul-wizard/interval-apps) monorepo, so fixes to the shared engine land in both apps
