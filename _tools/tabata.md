**Tabata Timer** is a free, open-source interval timer built with Flutter. It runs in the browser and on Android (RuStore, plus a preview APK), speaks English and Russian, and follows the classic protocol — 20 seconds of work, 10 seconds of rest, 8 rounds — with every number adjustable.

- Sound and vibration cues on phase change (vibration can be turned down or off)
- Keeps the screen on while the timer runs
- Light and dark theme that follows the phone until you pick one
- Part of the [interval-apps](https://github.com/manul-wizard/interval-apps) monorepo, so fixes to the shared engine land in both apps
